TAMM (Time, Autonomy, Mastery, Machine) is the framework `/theway:engineering` uses to find the obstacles a change can hit. It is installed at `~/.theway/standards/tamm-framework.md`.

## The four dimensions

| Dimension | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Time | Timeline pressure, dependencies and blocking, the critical path, competing priorities |
| Autonomy | Decision constraints, approval bottlenecks, organizational and policy dependencies, stakeholder alignment |
| Mastery | Skill gaps, unfamiliar technology, learning curves, knowledge transfer, key-person dependencies |
| Machine | Performance and scalability limits, infrastructure, legacy systems, security constraints, integration, technical debt |

Some risks span several dimensions, for example learning while delivering (Time and Mastery) or an architecture decision that needs approval (Autonomy and Machine).

## Risk levels

- **Low:** clear countermeasures exist, the team has experience, no blockers are expected.
- **Medium:** some uncertainty, limited experience, possible blockers that can be managed.
- **High:** unclear countermeasures, no experience, likely blockers or critical dependencies.

## The hardpoints table

Every `spec.md` has a "Hardpoints (TAMM Analysis)" section. It lists only Medium and High risks; Low risks are left out.

Columns: Category (one of the four dimensions), Risk (Medium or High), Impact, and numbered Countermeasures.

Countermeasures must be specific to the change, not generic. When a risk appears during implementation that the spec did not foresee, it goes into this table.
