A change is one unit of work: a folder with its spec and its tasks.

## Change IDs

A change ID is lowercase kebab-case and starts with one of four verbs: `add-`, `update-`, `refactor-` or `remove-`. For example `add-user-authentication` or `update-payment-api`.

[`theway validate-id`](../cli.mdx#validate-id) checks the format, and `/theway:engineering` runs it before it writes a change folder. A standalone ID must not end in a one- or two-digit number, since `-NN` is kept for series members.

## Series

A series is a set of dependent changes that share a `<series-id>`. Each member is `<series-id>-NN`, with a zero-padded two-digit ordinal starting at `01`, assigned in dependency order:

```text
add-user-auth-01
add-user-auth-02
add-user-auth-03
```

- A new member continues from the next free ordinal, counting active and archived members. Existing folders are never renamed or renumbered.
- With `--series`, topic suffixes (`add-user-auth-login`), letter suffixes (`-a`, `-b`), unpadded numbers (`-1`), `-00` and double ordinals are rejected.
- A series member is validated with `validate-id --series`.
- Each member's `spec.md` has a line under its title: `**Series:** <series-id> — NN of TOTAL, depends on <series-id>-(NN-1)`. The `01` member states no dependency.

`/theway:develop-workflow` takes the members in order and merges each PR before it starts the next change.

## Folder layout

`<project>` is the name of your project directory.

```text
~/.theway/<project>/changes/
├── add-redis-cache/
│   ├── spec.md      # required: why, what, risks and Success Criteria
│   ├── tasks.md     # required: numbered tasks
│   └── design.md    # optional: contracts, data models, diagrams
└── archive/
    └── 2025-10-15-update-auth-middleware/
```

In `tasks.md`, only numbered checkbox lines are tasks: `- [ ] 1. …` or `- [x] 2a. …`. Other checkboxes are not counted.

## Archive format

An archived change keeps its whole folder and is named `YYYY-MM-DD-<change-id>`, with the local date of the archive:

```text
~/.theway/<project>/changes/archive/
├── 2025-10-10-add-logging-framework/
└── 2025-10-15-update-auth-middleware/
```
